About

A drinking club with a running problem.

What is hashing?

Hashing is a non-competitive social run. A hare lays a trail, the pack follows it, and everyone meets at the end for the circle.

Hash Home

The Hash Home for the Sunday runs is the Premiere Hotel. Jeepneys leave from Premiere Hotel.
